## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an immaculate, reader-first news reading environment tailored for mobile syndication. Drawing inspiration from contemporary digital journalism and premium mobile editorial interfaces, it balances information velocity with calm, uncluttered ergonomics. 

### Brand Personality & Emotional Intent
- **Credible & Authoritative:** The interface establishes trust instantly through crisp typography, sharp content hierarchy, and disciplined structural alignment.
- **Distraction-Free Focus:** Backgrounds lean into clean pure whites and atmospheric slate-50 washes, allowing imagery and headline text to anchor reader attention.
- **Swift & Responsive:** Clean micro-interactions, tactile pill tags, and rapid feed diagnostics provide readers and feed curators with palpable control.

### Design Style: Modern Editorial Minimalism
The aesthetic pairs classic broadsheet typographic rigor with fluid, app-native ergonomics:
- **Card-Led Modular Surfaces:** Distinct card blocks with generous radii (16px–20px) frame individual news items, preventing feed fatigue.
- **Refined Materiality:** Subtle, ambient multi-layer drop shadows provide lift above slate-50 foundation planes without visual clutter.
- **Micro-Contrast Accents:** Rich, deep teal anchors navigational intent, while focused amber accents elevate real-time trending topics and active signals.

## Colors

The color system establishes a disciplined editorial atmosphere, prioritizing legibility and visual hierarchy over decorative saturation.

### Role Allocations & Strategy
- **Primary (`#0F766E` - Deep Teal):** Used intentionally for top-tier actions, active bottom navigation states, primary category chips, and key interactive highlights. It conveys depth, stability, and intellectual authority.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Warm Amber):** Reserved for high-urgency editorial cues, "Trending" badges, bookmark highlights (`★`), and breaking news badges.
- **Tertiary (`#0284C7` - Sky Blue):** Applied to secondary utility touchpoints, feed validation indicators, and link breakout prompts (`[ Read original article ↗ ]`).
- **Neutral Palette (`#0F172A` down to `#F8FAFC`):** Anchored by rich slate tones. `#0F172A` delivers high-contrast readability for article titles, `#475569` handles body previews and metadata, while `#F8FAFC` provides the foundational canvas contrasting cleanly against `#FFFFFF` elevated cards.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy blends the bold, geometric warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and interactive elements with the engineered legibility of **Inter** for continuous body text, feed summaries, and technical feed logs.

### Application Guidelines
- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Use tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) and heavy weights (`700`–`800`) to create high-contrast editorial impact for article titles and screen anchors.
- **Article Summaries & Content (`Inter`):** Maintain generous line height (`1.5` to `1.65`) for effortless scanning of Vietnamese and multilingual editorial feeds.
- **Metadata (`caption` & `label-sm`):** Standardize feed attribution strings (`Source • Category • Timestamp`) in crisp slate-500 tones to preserve visual order.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is optimized for single-handed mobile engagement while seamlessly scaling to tablet and desktop readouts.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Mobile Handheld (360px - 480px):** Single-column fluid stream with fixed outer margins of `16px` (`margin-mobile`). Vertical article cards stack with `12px` to `16px` gaps. Content renders edge to edge with no top bar, anchored by a 64px fixed bottom navigation bar with safe-area insets.
- **Tablet / Large Screens (768px+):** Fluid 6-column or split 2-column view where article previews coexist alongside the active feed reader frame or RSS source panel.
- **Rhythm & Padding:** Component interiors rely strictly on `space-md` (16px) for compact cards and `space-lg` (20px) for featured hero cards. Grouped elements like source avatars and timestamps adhere to `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined physical layering model of warm, ambient shadows and subtle borders, avoiding flat monotony or stark brutalism.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F8FAFC` base surface for page backgrounds and content scrolls.
- **Level 1 (Feed Cards & Source Items):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface paired with an ultra-soft drop shadow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`) and a delicate boundary stroke (`1px solid #E2E8F0` at 60% opacity).
- **Level 2 (Active Overlays & Floating Top Bars):** Semi-translucent frosted surfaces (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) elevated by `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Bottom Navigation Bar):** The fixed bottom navigation sits elevated with `0 -4px 20px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, framed by a crisp top divider (`#E2E8F0`). Modals and confirmation dialogs utilize an ambient wash (`0 20px 35px -8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.16)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces friendly, modern, and ergonomic rounded geometries that echo contemporary mobile device hardware.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Cards & Containers:** Standardized at `16px` (`1rem`) to `20px` (`1.25rem`) corner rounding. This creates a soft, tactile boundary for image thumbnails and content groups.
- **Interactive Tags & Category Chips:** Strictly pill-shaped (`9999px` full radius), making them immediately recognizable as tap targets in horizontal filter scrollers.
- **Action Buttons & Inputs:** Form fields, text inputs, and action buttons utilize a `12px` to `14px` radius for structural balance.
- **Media Thumbnails:** Featured article thumbnails use an internal border radius matching the parent container inset (`12px`–`14px`), while inline thumbnail lists utilize a fixed `12px` radius.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., `+ Add RSS Source`, `Validate RSS`):** Filled deep teal (`#0F766E`) with white text, font `label-lg`, height `44px`–`48px`, `12px` radius. Subtle active scale transition (`scale(0.98)`).
- **Secondary / Ghost (e.g., `Read original article ↗`, `Cancel`):** Slate-100 background (`#F1F5F9`) with slate-700 text (`#334155`), transitioning to low-opacity teal borders upon hover/focus.

### Chips & Category Pills
- **Filter Chips (`All`, `Thời sự`, `Công nghệ`):** Pill shape (`9999px`), horizontal auto-scroll container.
  - *Active State:* Background `#0F766E`, text `#FFFFFF`, bold weight.
  - *Inactive State:* Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid transparent`.
- **Trending Accent Badge:** Small pill with `#FEF3C7` background, `#D97706` text, and flame/trending micro-icon (`label-sm`).

### Article Cards
- **Featured Hero Card:** Vertical stack layout. Full-width `16:9` image thumbnail with `14px` radius, followed by metadata (`Source` • `Time`), high-contrast title in `headline-md`, brief two-line body excerpt, and quick-save bookmark toggle (`★` / `☆`).
- **Compact List Card:** Horizontal split layout. Left: metadata and 2-line title in `headline-sm`. Right: square `72x72px` rounded image thumbnail (`12px` radius). Bottom: source badge and relative time.

### Bottom Navigation Bar
- **Container:** Height `64px` plus safe-area-bottom padding. Background `#FFFFFF` with blur backdrop on transparent browsers.
- **Items:** 4 primary destinations: `Home` (🏠), `Explore/Search` (🔍), `Bookmarks` (🔖), `RSS Sources` (📡).
- **State Styling:** Active item features primary teal icon and label (`label-sm`), inactive items render in muted slate (`#94A3B8`).

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Search Input:** Height `44px`, pill or rounded `12px`, background `#F1F5F9`, left-aligned search glyph, slate-400 placeholder text. Focus triggers a `2px` outer ring in `#0F766E` with background shifting to `#FFFFFF`.

### RSS Source Card
- **Structure:** White card (`16px` radius) with source logo/favicon (`36x36px`), feed name in `label-lg`, URL in `caption`, and status indicator dot:
  - Active: `#16A34A` (●)
  - Error: `#DC2626` (● Fetch Error)
  - Fetching: Animated teal spinner (◌)
